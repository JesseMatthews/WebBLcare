Privacy Policy

How we use information you share with Bloomhaven Care through this website.

Last updated: 13 May 2026

Information we collect

When you contact Bloomhaven Care, we may collect the details you choose to send us, including your name, email address, phone number, enquiry type, message, and any information needed to respond appropriately.

How we use your information

  • To respond to care, assessment, referral, or recruitment enquiries.
  • To arrange a free home assessment or follow-up conversation when requested.
  • To keep a clear record of website enquiries and our responses.
  • To meet relevant care, safeguarding, quality, and administrative responsibilities.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information where it is needed to respond to your enquiry, provide requested services, meet legal duties, or protect someone from harm.

Website forms

Messages submitted through the website may be processed by FormSubmit so they can be delivered to our email inbox. You can also contact us directly by phone or email if you prefer.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete information where we no longer need to keep it. Some care-related records may need to be retained for legal, safeguarding, or regulatory reasons.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email info@bloomhavencare.co.uk or call 07436 125564.
