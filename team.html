A Day in the Life

What does a Bloomhaven carer actually do?

No two days are ever the same — that's what makes this work so rewarding. Here's a typical day for one of our carers:

7:30 AM

Morning Briefing

Check care plans, notes from previous visits, and any updates from the coordinator.

8:00 AM

First Morning Visit

Assisting a client with their morning routine — personal care, breakfast, medication.

10:30 AM

Companionship Visit

A longer social visit — a cup of tea, conversation, a puzzle, or a walk in the garden.

1:00 PM

Lunch & Meal Preparation

Preparing a hot, nutritious meal for a client and sharing it with them over conversation.

4:00 PM

Afternoon & Evening Rounds

More visits — personal care, light housekeeping, medication, and settling clients in for the evening.
