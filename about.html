Our Story

Built from experience and heart

Bloomhaven Care was founded in 2026 by a team of experienced care workers who shared a frustration and a vision. Having worked across the sector for years, they saw how care could be done differently — not built on targets and tick-boxes, but on trust, consistency, and genuine human connection.

They set out to build the agency they wished had existed for their own loved ones — one where carers are valued, clients are truly listened to, and quality is never compromised. That is Bloomhaven Care.

As a growing team, we are focused on supporting people across Preston and nearby communities — while keeping the family feel that shaped Bloomhaven from the beginning.

Our Approach

Person-centred care, always

We believe care should be built around the individual — not around what's easiest for a provider. Every care plan begins with listening. Really listening.

We take time to understand what matters most to each person: their routines, their preferences, their joys, and their goals. Only then do we design their care.
